# Data Use Agreement

This document sets out the conditions under which the candidate dataset for the YAIL GenAI Literacy mini-analysis may be used. Please read it in full before downloading or opening the data.

## 1. Purpose and scope

The dataset is a reduced educational copy of anonymous survey data collected for a scientific study, and it is provided to you **solely for the purpose of completing this assignment**.

These terms apply to:

- The dataset file itself, in whole or in part
- All derived artifacts — figures, tables, aggregates, models, notebooks, scripts, and reports
- Any interaction with third-party or AI tools that contains the data or derived material

The candidate dataset is not the complete research dataset and must not be used as an authoritative basis for publication.

## 2. Permitted use

You may:

- Analyse the data locally for the purpose of this assignment
- Store it on your own personal device, or in your own **private** repository

## 3. Prohibited use

You may not:

- Publish, redistribute, or share the dataset or any subset or row-level extract of it
- Upload it to public repositories, forums, cloud shares, public datasets, or public AI playgrounds
- Use the data for any purpose beyond this assignment
- Attempt to identify, single out, or contact respondents
- Combine the data with external sources in an attempt to identify individuals
- Scrape, extend, or supplement the dataset with additional collected data

## 4. Data integrity

- Do not fabricate, alter, or selectively delete records in order to change or strengthen a result
- Document every transformation you apply — filtering, recoding, imputation, exclusion — in your report
- Your findings must be reproducible from the code you submit

Honest reporting of a messy or inconclusive result is valued more highly than a tidy result that the data does not support.

## 5. AI tools and third-party services

Using AI assistants for this work is permitted and is not a disadvantage. It does not, however, change your confidentiality obligations:

- Do not paste raw data rows into public, shared, or training-enabled AI sessions
- Any AI conversation link containing project data, analysis, or findings must remain private
- Disclose your tool use in the AI-use note described in the README

The same applies to any other third-party service — online notebooks, chart builders, spreadsheets, or file converters.

## 6. Confidentiality of results

Your analysis, findings, figures, and generated outputs remain confidential. They must not be made public, published, or shared outside the review process until YAIL has provided explicit written permission.

## 7. Retention and deletion

- Delete all local copies of the dataset and any derived data upon request from YAIL
- In any case, delete them once the review process concludes if you are not selected
- Confirm deletion in writing if asked to do so

## 8. Incident reporting

If the data is exposed accidentally — pushed to a public repository, included in a shared link, sent to the wrong recipient, or held on a lost or stolen device — inform us **immediately** at **office@youngaileaderslinz.at**. Prompt disclosure is treated as responsible handling, not as a failure.

Completing the consent checkbox in the application form constitutes your agreement to these terms.
